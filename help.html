Need a hand?

Relay is deliberately simple. Here are the basics.

Looking for work

Create a profile, keep your skills and location accurate, browse live openings, then apply. Your age is used for the 18+ access rule and is not shown in the employer talent directory.

Hiring

Complete an employer profile, publish a clear opening, review applications, and contact people from Relay when there is a genuine reason to continue the conversation.

Something feels wrong

Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank credentials, or money because someone asked inside a Relay conversation. Report the relevant job or profile and review the Safety and Community Rules pages.

Account access

Use Forgot password to start recovery. Google sign-in is available once the Google provider is enabled for Relay.
