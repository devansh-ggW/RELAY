Trust & safety

Use Relay like a real marketplace.

Clear information, sensible boundaries, and no pretending.

Verify before you commit

Review the employer, company, role, pay, location, and communication carefully. If something does not add up, pause and verify it independently.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, banking credentials, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary job applications or messages.

Watch for suspicious requests

Be cautious if someone asks you to pay money to obtain a job, buy unusual equipment from a specific seller, move a conversation to an unrelated service immediately, or provide sensitive information before there is a legitimate hiring reason.

Meet responsibly

If a real-world meeting is necessary, use sensible precautions and choose an appropriate public or professional setting. Tell someone you trust where you are going.

Be honest

Profiles, qualifications, job descriptions, company information, compensation, and applications should be truthful. Impersonation and deliberate deception are not allowed.

Age requirement

Relay currently serves adults 18 and over. Do not create an account or misrepresent your age to access the service.

Report problems

Relay's reporting and moderation tools should be used when a listing, profile, or message appears fraudulent, abusive, unsafe, or otherwise against the rules. We will expand these tools before public launch.

Important limitation

Relay cannot independently verify every statement made by every user. The safest approach is to treat marketplace information as user-provided until you have independently verified what matters to you.

If something feels wrong, you do not owe anyone a response. Stop the conversation and use the reporting route when available.
