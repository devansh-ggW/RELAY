Complaints

Something wrong? Tell us.

Relay will maintain a complaint and grievance process for reports about accounts, job listings, messages, privacy, or other platform issues.

What to include

Give us the page or account involved, what happened, when it happened, and any information needed to locate the relevant content. Avoid sending passwords, one-time codes, or other unnecessary secrets.

What happens next

We will acknowledge and review complaints according to the process applicable to the service and the nature of the complaint. Some matters may require additional information before a decision can be made.
