Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Relay may collect when you create an account, publish a profile or job, apply for a role, or communicate through the service.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use Relay, this can include your name, email address, account credentials handled by our authentication provider, age confirmation, city, state, skills, headline, profile description, job information, applications, messages, and other content you choose to submit.

3. Authentication

Relay uses Supabase Auth for account authentication. If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles the Google authentication step and Relay receives the information made available through that authentication flow. Relay does not receive your Google password.

4. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate profiles, publish and search job openings, process applications, support conversations, prevent abuse, maintain the service, respond to requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Public and user-visible information

Information you intentionally publish as part of a profile or job listing may be visible to other Relay users. Do not publish passwords, government identification numbers, bank credentials, private contact information, or other information that you do not want exposed through a marketplace profile or listing.

6. Age information

Relay currently limits the service to adults 18 and over. We collect an age declaration and related onboarding record to operate that eligibility rule. Do not provide false age information.

7. Service providers

Relay may use infrastructure and service providers needed to operate authentication, databases, hosting, security, communications, and other core functionality. Providers receive information only as needed for their role and under their applicable terms and privacy commitments.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the account and service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce rules, and maintain security. Specific retention periods may change as Relay's operations mature.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their credentials and avoid publishing sensitive information.

10. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information where available under applicable law and Relay's operational capabilities. Account deletion and other privacy controls will be made available through Relay as the product matures.

11. Children

Relay is not currently intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly design the service for children. If we learn that an account has been created by someone under the eligibility age, we may restrict or remove that account and take appropriate steps regarding associated data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as Relay's data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

13. Contact

Before public launch, Relay should publish a dedicated privacy contact address for privacy requests and legal notices.

This page is a product-ready draft, not legal advice. Because Relay will process personal data in India, have the final policy and actual data flows reviewed for compliance before launch.
