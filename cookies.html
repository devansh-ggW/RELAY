Cookie & storage notice

Last updated: 22 September 2026

What Relay uses

Relay uses browser storage and authentication session mechanisms needed to keep you signed in and operate the application. Supabase Auth may use cookies, local storage, or related browser mechanisms depending on the authentication flow and browser.

Essential storage

Essential storage helps maintain your authenticated session, security state, and application functionality. Relay does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels as part of the product.

Future analytics

If Relay adds non-essential analytics or marketing technologies, this notice and the applicable consent controls will be updated before those technologies are used.

Managing storage

You can control or clear browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing essential storage can sign you out or affect application functionality.
