Community rules

Use Relay like a professional.

These rules keep the marketplace useful and safer for everyone. They are part of the Relay Terms of Service.

1. Be honest

Do not impersonate another person or company. Do not publish jobs, qualifications, portfolios, compensation, or work history that you know to be materially false or misleading.

2. No under-18 accounts

Relay is currently restricted to people aged 18 and over. False age information is prohibited.

3. No scams or unsafe requests

Do not use Relay to request passwords, one-time codes, financial account access, money transfers, or other sensitive credentials. Do not create jobs intended to facilitate fraud, theft, harassment, or other unlawful conduct.

4. Respect the other side

No threats, targeted harassment, discriminatory abuse, or repeated unwanted contact. Keep conversations relevant to the work.

5. Keep job posts clear

State the role, expected work, location/work style, and compensation as accurately as reasonably possible. Do not bait users with one offer and switch them to something materially different.

6. Reporting

Use Relay's reporting and complaint channels when available. Relay may remove content or restrict accounts when it reasonably believes the rules have been broken.

Launch note: Relay will publish its real complaint contact and operator details before public launch. Do not treat placeholders as contact information.
